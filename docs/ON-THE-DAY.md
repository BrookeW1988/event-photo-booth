# On the day

## The week before

- [ ] Booth is live, and you've taken a test photo **on the iPad** at the live address
- [ ] Your links file (`my-links.md`) is on your laptop and phone
- [ ] Ask the venue for the wi-fi name and password, and whether guest wi-fi blocks anything. Bring a phone hotspot as a backup
- [ ] Stand or tripod mount for the iPad (a floor stand at face height is best), plus a long charging cable
- [ ] A small sign: "Photo booth! Tap the screen to start" with your hashtag

## Set up the iPad (15 minutes)

1. **Charge it**, and keep it plugged in all day.
2. **Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never.**
3. **Settings → Apps → Safari → Camera → Allow** (on older iPads: Settings → Safari → Camera).
4. Connect to the venue wi-fi.
5. Open the live **admin** page on your laptop and scan its QR code with the iPad camera. The booth opens in Safari.
6. Tap through one photo to say yes to the camera. Delete the test photo from the admin page afterwards.
7. **Lock it to the booth with Guided Access:** Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On, and set a passcode. Back in Safari, triple-click the top button (or the home button) → Start. Now guests can't leave the booth. Triple-click and enter the passcode to get out.

## Light and placement

- Light on faces, not behind them. Don't put the booth in front of a window.
- A plain or pretty wall behind (a balloon wall, a sequin backdrop, florals) makes every photo better.
- Leave room for 3 or 4 people to squeeze in.
- **Back camera** (the default) takes sharper photos, but guests can't see the screen. Perfect if a helper runs it. **Selfie camera** lets guests see themselves: add `?cam=front` to the booth link before the `#`.

## The big screens

- **Photo wall** (`/wall#key=...`): open on a TV or laptop plugged into one. It checks for new photos every 45 seconds and spotlights one every 8 seconds. Full screen: Ctrl+Cmd+F on a Mac, F11 on Windows.
- **Slideshow** (`/slideshow#key=...`): for the end of the night. It plays every photo from the day, oldest first, and keeps adding new ones.
- Both use the booth key, so guests at home can't see them.

## During the event

- The booth goes back to the start screen by itself after a minute of no touches, so a half-finished guest never blocks the next one.
- Photos stay on the iPad until they're saved, so a wi-fi blip just means "trying again".
- A bad photo? Open the admin page on your phone and tap **Delete**. It disappears from the wall within a minute (and from everywhere within about 10 minutes).
- Booth stuck on something odd? Triple-click out of Guided Access, reload the page, start Guided Access again.

## After the event

1. Admin page → **Download guest list (CSV)**. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
2. Only add people marked **"yes" to updates** to your mailing list. Everyone else gave their email just to get their photos.
3. Want a few photos for your own socials? Ask the guests first. The photos are theirs.
4. When you're done, admin page → **Delete everything**. It clears every photo and email. Guests who saved their photos keep them, but their links stop working.
