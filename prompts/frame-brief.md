Artwork for an event photo booth frame. Portrait, 2:3 shape (taller than it is wide).

Three guest photos and the event's name will be placed ON TOP of this artwork later, covering most of the middle. So:
- Put the decoration around the outside: a border, the corners, motifs scattered near the edges.
- Keep the middle 70% calm: a soft texture, gradient or plain colour, with no important detail there.
- No words, letters, numbers or logos anywhere. The booth adds the event name itself.
- No people, faces or hands.
- No empty boxes, photo frames or placeholder rectangles.
- Full bleed: the art goes right to every edge, but keep anything important about 8% in from the edges, because they get trimmed a little.
- If reference images are attached, match their colours and style.
